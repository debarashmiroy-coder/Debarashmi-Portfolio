০৪ পরিচয়Introduction

আমার গল্প My Story —my story.

I love untangling messy problems and turning them into experiences that just make sense. With 6+ years in SaaS product design, I'm currently designing products at DoSelect (Info Edge), where I've shipped everything from AI-powered assessment creation and AI interviews to proctoring experiences and recruiter workflows.

I'm happiest at the intersection of UX, systems thinking, and a little bit of AI magic — asking "Why does this exist?" before "How should it look?" Whether it's simplifying a complex enterprise workflow or shaping a product from scratch, I enjoy finding elegant solutions to complicated problems.

I believe good design isn't about adding more — it's about knowing what to take away. I rely on user research, data, and healthy curiosity to build products that feel intuitive, scalable, and genuinely useful. If there's a smarter way to solve a problem, you'll probably find me sketching it on a whiteboard (or convincing Figma to do something it probably shouldn't).

অনুভূতি Feeling — "Life is a bundle of problems, and you need to solve it anyhow" ~ that's me, I said it...
